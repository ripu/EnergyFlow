Read straight from the inverter, every five seconds. No cloud, no account.

v2.0.0 · 9 August 2026

The problem

To know what the system was doing, you had to trust someone else.

  • The manufacturer's portal lives in the cloud. A reading from “now” means waiting, and it comes back through a company's server.
  • Household data leaves the house. Consumption, timings, habits: when the lights go on.
  • And the numbers shown were not the real ones. Not a delay: the wrong quantities altogether, wearing the face of the right ones.

What it does

Reads the inverter, shows the flow, and admits when it doesn't know.

  • Reads the inverter directly, every five seconds. No middleman, over the home network: the protocol is the one the device already speaks.
  • Shows where the energy goes — sun, house, battery, grid. On a wall panel readable from three metres, and on the same page from a phone or a Mac.
  • Admits when it doesn't know. Stale, frozen, untrustworthy — three faces.

Who it is for

Four actors, and only one of them has anything to do.

Whoever walks past

The whole family. No interaction expected: you look at it, like a clock.

Whoever opens the page

From the sofa or from away, phone or Mac. Same page, no password to type.

Whoever maintains it

One person, who wants readable logs, an honest health status, and a check that fails when something is genuinely wrong.

The inverter

The source. It asks for nothing and does not know it is being read: it just answers.

In action

The same system, two screens.

What changed

Three numbers looked right. They were not.

“Today's energy: 4,568.6 kWh” That was lifetime production, since installation day.
“Battery voltage: 1,220.5 V” That was the energy discharged from the battery, in kWh — not volts.
“Grid exchange” It was reading a register holding the day's production.

And the solar figure always added up — even with the wrong map.

A calculation that cannot be wrong verifies nothing. Solar now comes from the panels' measured power, with twelve physical checks beside it that can fail.

State — v2.0.0

What is there today, and what still is not.

There

48 verified fields on register map v2.1 · persistent history at one-minute resolution, 90 days, with a per-day summary that is never deleted · 3D view · a theme that follows sunrise and sunset · portrait kiosk panel · token-protected access reachable only from the tailnet · three macOS widgets · twelve physical checks.

Not there

21 registers still unidentified, excluded rather than guessed · in the daily roll-up only production comes from the inverter's counter, the rest is reconstructed · no notifications or alarms: the panel is looked at, it does not call you · no control over the inverter: EnergyFlow reads, it does not drive.

Nothing in the right-hand column is presented anywhere else as if it already existed.

How to try it

Three ways, and no password to ask anyone for.

On the wall panel: it is already there, and it starts by itself.

From a phone or a Mac, at home or away, with Tailscale on:

https://<rpi-host>.<tailnet>.ts.net/

Locally, from the project folder:

python3 invert.py --serve
# → http://localhost:8003

Real credentials and addresses: see the password manager. Everything else is in the operating guide.
